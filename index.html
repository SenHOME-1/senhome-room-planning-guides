How to Use AI to Decorate a Room You Already Live In

By SenHOME

Use AI to compare furniture, textiles and wall colors in a room you know. Start with the room as it is, decide what stays, and keep the same view while changing one thing at a time.

1. Decide what you want to change

Write a one-sentence goal, such as making a bedroom feel calmer without replacing the bed. Photograph the area with its floor, windows and door openings visible. List the furniture you want to keep, and measure the room if you plan to move or replace a piece.

2. Make a baseline you can compare

In SenHOME Web, use an Inspiration template to explore an initial direction. Return the essential pieces you want to keep before comparing alternatives. A useful starting point represents the room you will actually live with, rather than an unrelated showroom.

3. Change one material or fabric

Select a surface and open Material to try a different finish. Hold the furniture, camera angle and lighting steady. For example, compare a patterned wall with a plain light finish while keeping the bedroom furniture and wood flooring in place. This makes it easier to see what the finish itself changes.

4. Compare one furniture replacement

Select the object, open Assets, and choose Model to explore an alternative. Compare the original and replacement from the same angle. Check width, depth and the walking route around the piece. A design preview helps you compare options; it does not replace real measurements.

5. Check the real room before ordering

View paint and fabric samples in daylight and at night. Measure the furniture, doorways and delivery path, and allow space for drawers, doors and a chair pulled away from a desk. Save your preferred version so you can compare it with the physical room.
